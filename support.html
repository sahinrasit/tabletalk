Help & FAQ

Need help or want to request a language? Write to mrrasitsahin@icloud.com. We answer within 48 hours.

How do I use it?

  1. Choose your language (bottom) and the other person's language (top).
  2. Lay the iPhone flat between you.
  3. Each person taps their own microphone and speaks. The other side shows the translation in large text.

Prefer to hold the phone? Switch to Hand over mode from the middle bar.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The first time you use a language pair, iOS downloads the language pack (Settings > Languages > Download). After that, translation and speech recognition work offline, even in airplane mode.

Why is voice input unavailable for my language?

Offline speech recognition depends on what Apple supports on your iPhone model and iOS version. If it isn't available, you can still type, and translations still work offline.

Which languages are supported?

All languages offered by Apple's on-device translation, including English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese.

What does Table Talk Pro include?

A one-time purchase that removes the 10-translations-per-day limit and unlocks all situation packs. There is no subscription. To restore it on a new iPhone, open Settings > Restore Purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
